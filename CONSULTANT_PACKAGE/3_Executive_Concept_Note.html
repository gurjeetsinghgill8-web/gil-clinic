Executive Concept Note & Strategic Brief

CardioQueue & GHOS v2.0

Autonomous Patient Flow, Multi-Specialty Clinical Copilot & Zero-Waiting Diagnostic Operating System for Clinics & Hospitals.

⚠️ 1. Identify Problem

Clinics suffer from overcrowded waiting rooms, slow prescription typing, siloed diagnostics (ECG, Echo, TMT), and expensive third-party SaaS fees with zero offline resilience.

💡 2. Suggest Solution

"Register Once, Track Everywhere". A local-first PWA where patients track wait times on their phones with vibrate buzzers, while doctors get voice-to-Rx AI copilot.

🚀 3. Salient Benefits

Cuts waiting times by 70%, reduces prescription time to 30 seconds, sends free automated WhatsApp updates, and operates 100% offline.

🧠 Inter-Speciality Consulting Tool for Medicos

Enables general physicians and specialists to practice tertiary-care precision medicine by cross-checking guidelines in real time:

  • Cardiology ↔ Nephrology: Auto-calculates eGFR-based dose adjustments for SGLT2i, ACEi, and ARNi to protect renal function.
  • Cardiology ↔ Endocrinology: Recommends optimal anti-diabetic medications with proven cardiovascular mortality benefit.
  • Contraindication Guard: Flags dangerous drug interactions (e.g. Amiodarone + QT-prolonging antimicrobials) before prescription sign-off.
