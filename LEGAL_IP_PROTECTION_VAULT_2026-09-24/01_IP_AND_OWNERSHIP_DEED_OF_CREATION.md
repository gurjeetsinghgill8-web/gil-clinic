## 3. SCOPE OF PROTECTED INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

The protected assets include, but are not limited to:

1. **Harness Engineering Architecture:** The decoupled tripartite architecture (FastAPI PWA UI Layer → Use-Case Command Harness Layer → Domain Entities & Repositories) preventing spaghetti dependencies in clinical environments.
2. **Zero-App Patient Flow OS (CardioQueue):** The queue tracking workflow utilizing mobile browser QR codes, local device token storage, and browser-native sound/vibration alerts without reliance on expensive third-party SMS or WhatsApp API aggregators.
3. **The FIR (Feature & Issue Registry) Protocol & Implementations:**
   - **FIR-01:** The clinical handwriting prescription OCR pipeline integrating browser-based Puter AI & server-side Groq Vision with automatic client-side image compression (<10MB ceiling) and array-content markdown extraction.
   - **FIR-02:** The Self-Learning Clinical Drug Bank and Auto-Fill System, persisting structured medication attributes (Brand, Strength, Form, Frequency, Timing, Salt/Composition, Use-Count ranking, and form-prefix stripping).
   - **FIR-03:** The Remote Patient Diagnostics & File Upload Architecture for home-monitored BP/Pulse and lab reports direct to doctor queue.
   - **FIR-04:** The Dual-Channel Authentication Gateway resolving blank-screen popup bugs in mobile/PWA environments.
4. **All Source Files, Database Schemas, and Documentation** in this repository.
